# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro 7 + Tailwind 4 (from CONTEXT.md)

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, and potential freelance clients looking for engineering talent.

## Product Purpose

A professional portfolio to showcase past work, share technical expertise via blog posts, and provide a point of contact for new opportunities.

## Positioning

Full-stack product engineering with a focus on business outcomes.

## Operating Context

Evaluated on desktop and mobile browsers by technical peers, recruiters, and clients assessing engineering capabilities, design sensibility, and past business impact.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Bilingual FR (default, `/`) and EN (`/en/`) using Astro 7 i18n routing.
- The existing hero section is frozen and must be preserved verbatim (markup, colors, GSAP fade-in animations).
- Must use existing Content Layer schema for `blog` and `projects`.
- Deployment via Firebase Hosting.
- Motion unified on GSAP; Astro View Transitions for routing navigation.
- Project cards use three variants: Metrics-first, Outcome narrative, and Before/after split.

## Brand Commitments

- Primary colors: `#0367ff` and `#0a1128` (overriding CONTEXT.md's palette strategy).
- Typography: Poppins for headings and Lato for body text (overriding CONTEXT.md's font strategy).
- Logo: Transparent logo asset provided.
